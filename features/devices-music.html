Room devices and music ​

The room's devices ​

Each display belongs to a room (a Home Assistant area). Its buttons show the room's lights, switches, fans and media players, and the assistant can control only those: each request carries the room's devices, and the display only carries out actions on them.

ActionForWith
turn_on, turn_off, togglelights, switches, fans
brightnesslightsvalue 0–100
media_play_pause, media_next_trackmedia players
volumemedia playersvalue 0–100
play_musicspeakers that play music by namequery, what to play

Off with the grown-ups' "See and control the room's devices" switch.

Music by name ​

"Play Sandstorm", "put on some relaxing piano", "play the Frozen soundtrack": play_music goes to a speaker in the display's room.

  • An Echo (Home Assistant's Alexa Devices integration) is told to play it from Spotify, by voice command, so it works without Spotify Premium.
  • A Music Assistant player gets music_assistant.play_media, which searches the music services you've added to Music Assistant for the best match.

The display marks such speakers "plays music by name" in the device list it sends, so the assistant knows where play_music works. Speech-to-text often mishears song names ("Place and Storm" for "play Sandstorm"), so the assistant makes its best guess and plays it, and they can just ask again.

Off with the grown-ups' "Play music" switch.

The speaker has to be in the room

A display only controls its own room's devices. To play music from the living room's display on a Music Assistant speaker, put that speaker's device in the living room area in Home Assistant.

To play a sound or a stream on the displays themselves, see Playing audio.
