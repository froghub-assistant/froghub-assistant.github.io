Grown-ups' settings ​

The admin page ​

<your site>/admin, behind the PIN. It has:

  • Displays: every Frog Hub that has ever checked in, with edit (name, room, mic, volume), show the welcome, wipe (erases its storage and starts it again as a new display) and forget. Displays pick up changes on their next poll, within 5 seconds.
  • Announcements and alarms: say something on the displays now, and see or cancel alarms and timers.
  • Assistant: what the assistant may use (below), and which lists it may use.
  • Memory: the assistant's memory files, and asking it to remember or forget something.
  • History: what it was asked and what it answered.
  • Voices: the wake word recordings.

There's a phone app for it too: see the Android apps.

What the assistant may use ​

Each switch applies to both brains:

Look things up onlineWeb search, the dictionary, Wikipedia and Wikidata
Remember things between conversationsIts memory
Check the weatherThe weather
Set alarms and timersAlarms and timers
See and control the room's devicesLights, switches, fans and speakers in the display's room
Play musicMusic on the room's speakers, and radio, sound clips and audio links on the displays
Know who it's talking toThe name of whoever the display is for
Show cards and pictures on the screenCards, pictures and recipes on screen
Pass messages on to the other Frog HubsMessages, announcements and scheduled jobs
Keep a log of questions and answersThe History tab (off by default)
Read and change the shared to-do and shopping listsLists, only the ones you share
Find recipes on recipeapi.iorecipeapi.io rather than TheMealDB, when there's a key

When something is switched off, the assistant is told, so it can say so kindly if asked.

History ​

History lists everything the assistant was asked and what it answered, newest first: questions at the displays (which one, who, and what it did or showed), the admin page's memory requests, announcements and scheduled jobs, and any that got no answer in time. It can be searched and cleared there.

It's off by default: switch it on on that tab or the Assistant tab. Switching it off keeps what's there until it's cleared (you're asked whether to clear it). While it's on, the assistant can search it too ("what was that recipe yesterday?"), apart from what grown-ups asked in admin mode or from the admin page.

It's kept in ~/.config/froghub/history.jsonl, readable only by Frog Hub's user, and only the newest 1,000 are kept.

Admin mode on a display ​

Typing the PIN on a display (the gear button) unlocks admin mode there for a while, checked on the server, so nobody gets it by saying they're a grown-up. In admin mode the assistant may also say what it remembers and change or delete memories (otherwise it only adds new ones), save house rules ("bedtime is 8pm on school nights", "no scary stories") and follow them afterwards even if a child asks otherwise, and talk plainly about how the display works.
