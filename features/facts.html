Sums, units and facts ​

So the assistant doesn't do maths in its head or guess at facts, it has tools for them.

Sums ​

calculate works sums out exactly: + − × ÷, powers, brackets, roots and a few functions. The sum is parsed, never run as code.

Units ​

convert_units converts length, weight, volume (British pints and gallons, US cups), temperature, speed, time and area.

Wikipedia ​

wikipedia looks things up on Wikipedia, or on Simple English Wikipedia for children. It gives the best match's summary, its picture (which show_on_screen accepts) and other matches, or with more, the article itself.

Wikidata ​

wikidata gives exact facts from Wikidata: population, area, capital, birthdays, heights, atomic numbers, boiling points, distance from Earth and so on. It skips names and disambiguation pages, and says when the name could mean something else.

Wikipedia and Wikidata are off with the grown-ups' "Look things up online" switch.
