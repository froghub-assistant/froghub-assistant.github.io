Frog HubA homemade smart display for children's tablets

Like an Echo Show or a Nest Hub, on the tablets you already have (even old Fire tablets), with your own assistant, your own rules and four personalities.

What it can do
For developers
🗣️

Talk to it

"Hey Jarvis", then ask. Wake word, speech-to-text and voices run in your house through Home Assistant, in Verity, Falsity, Lovity or Frog's voice.

💡

Music, radio and the room

Lights, switches, fans and speakers in the display's own room, music by name on an Echo or a Music Assistant speaker, internet radio and sound clips.

⏰

Alarms, lists and announcements

"Wake me at seven", "add milk to the shopping list", "tell the kitchen I'm hungry", "every school day at 7:45, tell them to get their shoes on".

🍝

Recipes, words and facts

Recipes by name or by what's in the fridge, exact sums and unit conversions, a dictionary, Wikipedia and Wikidata, with cards and pictures on the screen.

🧑‍🧑‍🧒

Grown-ups decide

A PIN-protected admin page and switches for everything it may use, from looking things up online to playing music. Displays for grown-ups drop the children's rules.

🏠

Yours, at home

Your own server (a Raspberry Pi is plenty) and your own brain, Claude Code on your Claude subscription or a model API, even a local one.
